I am a developer and analyst whose work sits at the intersection of business, information systems, and building things that ship. The foundation was game development — Unity, C# — where the feedback loop is immediate and unforgiving, and it expanded outward from there into data analytics, quantitative tooling, and low-latency engineering.

What holds it together is a preference for work that can be opened, read, and run by somebody else. Every project in this archive is documented, tested, and pushed with its assumptions stated — because a result you cannot reproduce is not yet a result.

Practice

More than twenty completed projects, each one documented, tested, and pushed to GitHub. The range runs from C++ matching engines and derivatives pricing to credit risk models, SQL pipelines, and interactive dashboards — but the standard is the same everywhere: state the method, show the numbers, leave the code readable.

Currently

Building a live systematic trading system with a documented risk engine — position sizing, drawdown controls, and the logging to prove what happened and why. Alongside it, deepening C++ and stochastic calculus, because the models are only as good as the maths underneath them.

Education
