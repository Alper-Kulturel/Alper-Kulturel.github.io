Eight files tracing a single question: what a price actually is, and how quickly you can prove it. The work moves between the mathematics of derivatives and the machinery that quotes them — closed-form Greeks on one side, price–time priority matching engines and wire-speed market data on the other.

Each repository is documented, tested, and pushed with its assumptions stated plainly: what the model holds true, where it breaks, and what the benchmarks measured.

File index 08 items
  • cpp-black-scholes C++17 · pybind11 · CMake Analytical option pricer exposing all five Greeks, with implied volatility solved by Newton–Raphson.
  • cpp-order-book C++17 · pybind11 Price–time priority matching engine with O(log n) add, O(1) cancel, and partial fills.
  • cpp-feed-handler C++17 · pybind11 · ITCH Low-latency market data parser for the ITCH binary protocol, benchmarked at p50 and p99.
  • options-pricing-engine Python · NumPy · SciPy Black–Scholes, Monte Carlo, and binomial trees compared side by side, with Greeks and a volatility surface.
  • pairs-trading Python · statsmodels · Plotly Cointegration-based market-neutral pairs strategy with ADF testing and a full backtest.
  • stock-backtesting-engine Python · pandas · Plotly Event-driven backtester covering momentum, mean reversion, MA crossover, and pairs, with transaction costs.
  • portfolio-optimization Streamlit · SciPy · Plotly Markowitz efficient frontier with Max Sharpe, Min Variance, and Risk Parity optimizers.
  • monte-carlo-risk-simulator Python · NumPy Ten thousand simulated futures producing VaR, CVaR, and drawdown distributions.
